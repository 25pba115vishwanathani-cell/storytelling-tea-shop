How Story Telling Tea Shop Started

Story Telling Tea Shop was founded on 24 December 2025 by Sanjeev, Javan and Vishwanathan.

Our idea was simple — create a tea shop that is more than just a place to drink tea.

We wanted to create a comfortable space where people can drink freshly prepared tea, meet friends, have meaningful conversations, read, write and share their stories.

Why We Started

In today's busy world, people need a place where they can slow down, enjoy a cup of tea and spend quality time with others.

That is the idea behind Story Telling Tea Shop. Every cup brings people together, and every person has a story to tell.
