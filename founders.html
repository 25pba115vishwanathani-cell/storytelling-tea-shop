Our Story

Story Telling Tea Shop was created with a simple idea: tea is more than just a drink. It is a way to bring people together, share conversations and create memorable moments.

Our three founders — Sanjeev, Vishwanathan and Javan — share the vision of creating a warm and comfortable space where every customer can enjoy freshly prepared tea and share their own story.

“Tea is more than a drink, it’s a connection of hearts and stories.”
